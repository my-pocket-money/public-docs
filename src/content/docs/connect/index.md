Pocket Connect lets a partner app link a customer's Pocket Money account and
act on it with the customer's authorization. The partner never holds Pocket
credentials: the customer approves the link in a Pocket-hosted flow, and every
call is made with a token issued for that link.

Public documentation for Connect is coming soon. If you are integrating today,
contact your Pocket representative for the integration guide.
